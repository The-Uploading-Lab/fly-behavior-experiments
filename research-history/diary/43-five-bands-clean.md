**Question.** Yesterday's card showed the selected body walking at real
cadence in clean tripod but standing on its feet 89% of the time where a
fly stands 50-83%. Could the duty conflict close without breaking
anything else?

**Method.** Three lanes attacked the swing from different sides under
the new selection file — a judge-maintained executable theta that
runners must build from, created after two gains were measured against
stale bases. A cross-lane replay disagreement on one seed forced an
arbitration: main plus the canonical dial reproduced one lane to the
digit, which meant the other lane's better-looking body came from a
deviation, not a mechanism.

**Result.** The deviation was a port defect — adhesion left on the old
schedule while the joints re-timed — and its owner named it, withdrew
nine seeds and their own best card, and re-measured on the canonical
engine: the swing fraction alone closes duty (0.823 in band, 4/4, path
+10.6%). The full card then came back clean: five bands, six seeds
each, retention kept, with swing moving from its band's edge to its
middle unasked. Five one-seed or under-sized wins died at their own
preregistered bars overnight — a motor-authority window, a gain boost,
true shunting, a male amplitude, and a cadence repair whose sized
confirmation read three reversals against four advances.

**Interpretation.** The command timing was the lever; the drive must be
computed FOR the re-timed movement, which is why one warp works and the
same warp applied after the drive does not. The remaining thin spot is
named: left yaw 0.376 deg/s over its floor, deterministic, guarded. The
hardening trade was declined because it spends a measured band on a
self-set floor.

**Problems.** The night's biggest gain candidate died with a bug, and
its owner's honesty is the only reason the record is clean. The male
amplitude did not replicate; the species question stays open. Cadence
on the champion lineage remains unimproved since f28 — the reset repair
that seemed to fix it was eight dice of bad luck, caught before a claim.
