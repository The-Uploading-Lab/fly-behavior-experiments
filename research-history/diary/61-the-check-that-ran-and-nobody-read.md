## The question that found it

Not a hypothesis. A piece of bookkeeping: when the title changes, which of the
things we have written down about this fly does anything actually re-check?

The registry holds 68 constraint rows, 47 of them about walking and standing.
The v3.7 card veto reads **16**. Most of the remainder need instruments a
walking card does not have, which is fine. Six do not: they are computed on
every card the project runs and mapped into nothing, because the veto's reader
takes a banded in-band string and those six are rules and circular means.

One of the six was a live false claim. `coord.R1c`, the contralateral Cruse
rule, read **PASS on qh1** on the board. On the reigning champion's own card it
reads **0 of 11**.

**`feco_polarity_mode` fell twice.** Lane A had already run it in isolation on

That leaves `proprioceptor_phasic_mode` and `delay_mode`.

recorded its provenance as `searched` and did not record on how many seeds,

## The prediction, and a false negative

Written into the registry at 05:26, before the block ran, so it could fail:
reverting the delay map recovers the rule to a majority and moves the phase row
into band by at least three of twelve.

The first read was a two-seed scout and it said no — 0 of 2. That was recorded
as not met and held open rather than withdrawn. On ten seeds the same arm reads
**5 of 8 paired, 6 of 9 on its own standing runs, with the phase row in band on
7 of 9 against a baseline of zero.** Both clauses met.

This project's three-seed line has been used all night against false positives.
This is the first time it caught the opposite error, and the lesson is
symmetric: a scout can talk you out of a real effect as easily as into a false
one.

## What the repair costs

Cadence falls on every pair of both arms tried, 0.36 to 1.64 Hz, against a
champion margin of **0.7273 Hz** over the floor. Ground speed falls 0.63 mm/s,
taking 1.50 to about 0.87 against a band that starts at 2.0. Qualifying goes
from 9 of 10 to 1 of 8.

Reverting **any** single one of wa1's four keys costs that cadence, including
the FeCO key which costs no coordination at all. So the package bought the step
rate jointly and is not separable by reversion. The one route that recovered the
rule while holding cadence near the floor is a reshape rather than a revert, and
it recovered the rule on every run while leaving the phase value outside the
band, which is a different quantity from the rule and the one the card grades.

## Two flies, each good at half of walking

The obvious question was what else the coordinating body has, and it had never
been asked, because qh1's card is on the retired body and predates half the
rows. Twelve fresh judge seeds, 12 of 12 standing:

Of 28 rows graded on both cards, **qh1 on the exact body passes 19 and the
champion 16**. Four pass only on qh1: both contralateral rules, the phase, and
footfall separation — a row that conflicts on every champion this project has
crowned and that nothing in this night's work predicted. One passes only on the

That headline is one-sided and was corrected within the hour. A pass count
treats every row both bodies miss as neutral, and five are. By distance from the
band the champion sits nearer on three of those, including ground speed and
straightness; qh1 on two, including the vertical bounce. Totals six to four, or
five to four dropping qh1's phase pass, which is inside its band by 0.0044.

So the fair statement is not a score. **The older body is better at how the legs
are placed relative to one another. The champion is better at how fast and how
straight the fly travels.** Those are two different capabilities and the
champion is genuinely better at one of them.

## The row is not organised by segment

Two routes reached this and neither was looking for it.

Reading the claw asymmetry per leg pair against the phase deficit gave an
ordering that was exactly inverted: the most asymmetric pair had the phase
closest to the animal. Then lane E's symmetrised encoder changed 44 of 52 front
claw cells, 12 of 63 hind and **zero of 47 middle** — and the middle pair is the

An inverted per-segment ordering is what a cord-wide effect looks like when you
read it per segment. Whatever fixes this row acts on the cord, not on a leg,
which is consistent with the five independent dials lane A has now shown move
this fly's gait and leave the cord's single rhythm line untouched.

## And the line itself is at the animal's fifth percentile

The obvious next question, asked at 05:49 and answered by lane A at 06:12 from
the reference already on disk: is the cord's number the animal's number?

Over all **28,059 reference sequences**, the animal's step line medians
**10.00 Hz**, quartiles 7.50 to 12.50, with 8.5 per cent of sequences falling
within half a hertz of the model's 5.818.

So the cord's frequency is **58 per cent of the animal's median and sits at the
animal's own 5th percentile**. The cadence clause's floor is 5.0 and the
animal's 5th percentile is 5.0. **This fly steps at the rate of the slowest
twentieth of real flies and passes a clause written to admit them.** The cadence
row is not a row this model has satisfied; it is a row the band is wide enough
to hide.

**Narrowed at 06:18, four minutes after this entry landed, by lane A against its
own packet.** The five dials shown not to move the line are the tip cadence, the
joint range caps, the adaptation STRENGTH, the change of champion and the command
rate, and **not one of them is a timescale**: `adapt_a` is the size of the
adaptation current, `tau_w` is how fast it decays, and they are different keys.
The line's period is 172 ms and the champion's `tau_w` is 271 ms, so the obvious
candidate was untested and is now under test. Whether this frequency is a
property of the connectivity or of one time constant is OPEN. The judge had
already put the over-broad form into a report, which had not sent and was
corrected.

That is now `gait.cord_rhythm_frequency`, its own row rather than a note on the
cadence row, because a line reading "satisfied" beside a quantity 42 per cent
low is what the zero-conflicts aim exists to prevent. It is opened with a debt
attached: the project now holds three numbers for the animal's step rate — this
10.0, the cadence band's 5 to 16, and a recorded population median of 2.5 that
its own note says includes non-walking time. They are not one measure and the
reconciliation is owed before this row is cited as the animal's step rate. The
conflict is recorded now because a factor of 1.7 does not turn on which of them
is right.

**Three corrections to the section above, all made within the hour and two of
them against myself (07:09).**

**The frequency is not structural, and that question is closed.** The section
leaves it open between the connectivity and one time constant. Lane A halved

**The CONFLICT status is withdrawn, because I manufactured it.** The row was

**And the finding is sharper for losing its band.** The model's line is inside

**The three-numbers debt is mostly discharged**, and not by the lane that owed

## What it costs the bar, stated without a rule change

The qualifying clause orders on cadence. Contralateral phase is graded by the
card veto, but a veto only holds a challenger that already won the count and
never promotes one that lost it. The Cruse rule is graded by nothing. So a body
that improves the last two and loses the first is measured accurately on all
three and cannot crown.

**The bar was not changed.** Adding a route by which a body that steps less can
crown is a loosening, and a judge may tighten a rule it is invested in and may
not loosen one. It also decides what the title means, which is the class of
decision Robin took himself on 2026-08-18. It goes to him with a default of no
change.
