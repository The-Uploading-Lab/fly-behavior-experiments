The night's chain, each step registered before it ran: the fly is not
slow (instantaneous speed 6.1–9.7 mm/s, inside the real band) — it
travels 13–19× further than it gets (net/path 0.052–0.073, verified
independently by the judge). It is not wandering (heading stable). It
is not slipping in a way that matters (halving slip with real friction
left speed flat, and a controller re-fitted to grippy feet stands
8/12 on the judge's own seeds). It is not commanded to vibrate (the
network orders smooth sweeps; the body reverses 47% of ticks anyway).
The joints do what they are told. The plant is not a resonator. What
remains: **the network executes steps and does not repeat them** —
per-leg period CV ~0.9 against every structural lever tried
(adhesion, load-loop, motor-map boost 14×, denser connectome), none
of which moved it.

Inside the network the lane found a real rhythm — 40 motor rows
locked to the step cycle, leading movement by 35 ms — and then, in
the experiment that defines this project's standard, silenced it
against 40 randomly chosen rows and found the costs identical. The
rhythm is real, leading, addressed, and functionally inert under the
measured motor map. Twenty-six of the lane's own written-in-advance
predictions were right, thirty-five wrong, and every wrong one is in
the packet with its correction. Lane B, meanwhile, closed a dozen
sensory-channel families on legal seeds (claw at rest, club weak on
both animals), logged two fresh male-female disagreements, and
declared its own instrument family saturated — an honest stop.

The throne stays vacant under the new v3.1 bar. The next thetas are
being selected at measured input noise, and the next conflict to
close has a name now: make the network repeat itself.
