12:2x EEST. **The foot.** Since 2026-09-11 this lab has known that the champion drags its
left hind leg: it stands 0.35 mm deeper than the others, and yesterday lane B measured the
consequence on a frame-free stage, the left hind tip travels 0.079 mm of vertical distance
against the right hind's 0.376 on 217 of 217 control runs, one leg and not one side, since
the middle pair leans the other way. This morning B's archive scan ranked every family it
had ever run by how far that foot travels, and eight of the eleven best were experiments
that turned the claw proprioceptors down or changed their code. None had been run for that
foot, and none had a paired control, so B called it a ranked place to start and said it had
no clock for the direct test. It found the clock.

**What the claw sensors are supposed to do.** The femoral chordotonal organ has three
afferent classes. In the animal the claw axons carry joint position and stay active across
behaviours, while the hook and club axons are suppressed during walking (Mamiya 2018;
Dallmann 2025). The champion has this backwards on all three: claw is high-passed into a
movement signal and hook and club are left tonic. The registry carries that as a conflict
candidate on the row sensory.feco_claw_hook_club_state, and no one had paid to fix it.

**The experiment.** Twelve fresh seeds, three arms each, one base. The control is the
champion itself (its theta digest is the base's). The clawhind arm restores the claw code to
tonic on the hind legs only; the clawtonic arm does it on all six. Paired on the eleven seeds
where both arms stand, clawhind lifts the left hind's travel from 0.068 to 0.132 mm and its
lift count from 10 to 14.5 in five seconds, up on ten of eleven seeds (sign test p 0.0117),
with standing at twelve of twelve against the control's eleven, and forward speed unresolved
(five seeds faster, six slower). The right hind gives back a quarter of what the left gains,
so the pair converges rather than the body bobbing less. The whole-body arm moves the foot
further, resolves less, and costs a fifth of the speed.

**Why it is not crowned.** Three reasons, each B's own. The hind pair still fails the
amplitude gate this lane wrote this morning: the weak foot now clears the 0.10 mm floor but
the two feet's ratio reads 0.38 against a bar of 0.5, so hind alternation is still not a
number this body produces. The feet land further apart: footfall separation goes from 1.094,
inside the animal's band, to 1.178, outside it, on eleven of eleven seeds. And the stage is
world-vertical tip height, so part of the gain could be the body sitting 0.035 mm lower,
though the lift count, which is independent, moves the same way. Body height and its
oscillation both move toward the animal. A twelve-seed block is a screen; the registration
said so before it ran.

**The judge's ruling.** Accepted as the first paired fresh-seed result of this shift that
moves the model's blocked foot, on the animal's side of a registered conflict. Not a nominee
today: no title match can end before the 13:00 deadline, and a nominee has to pass the noon
guard, where the champion itself refuses. Clawhind goes to the next shift as a nominee
candidate: a bit-identity probe, the graded map folded in (the bind: compliant bodies stood
19 and 16 of 24 against 22), the title battery on sealed seeds, footfall separation read as
the known cost. B's next arm is already registered and unrun: the whole measured row, claw
tonic on every leg and hook and club returned to the high-pass.

**Correction, 14:2x EEST, on lane B's 12:34 packet.** The ruling above read the map fold as a
formality. It is not. The theta measured here carries the champion's bare 24 graded rows and
the noon guard refuses it; folded to the 16,583-row map it passes, but on one seed the fold
moves the champion itself from 5.80 to 6.49 mm of travel and the clawhind body from 6.50 to

fold and not the engine. The eleven paired seeds were measured on a body the guard refuses and
