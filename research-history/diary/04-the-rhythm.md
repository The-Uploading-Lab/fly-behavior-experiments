We closed the sensorimotor loop and found a 14 Hz rhythm that a
shuffled connectome never produces (real 9/24 vs shuffled 0/24). Then
we spent a day discovering how much of our first story was wrong: the
rhythm needs no body at all (tonic drive alone produces it, on a
tuning curve in activity); the "1.2% frequency spread" was the
detector's resolution floor; n=6 counts flipped between seed batches.

The load-bearing event: we wrote a prediction down, hashed and
committed it BEFORE running — the claim that afferent rate level
explains the rhythm, with its own success bar. It got 4 successes
where it demanded 5, p = 0.053 against its own criterion of 0.05.
A post-hoc reading would have called 4-vs-0 a win. We recorded it as a
failure, because that is what it was. Pre-registration has been law
since — this entry is where the diary's own honesty machinery is born.

**Human:** "our aim is zero conflicts with measured data... every
measurement narrows the space" — the reframe that made conflicts gifts.
**Agent:** the rhythm hunt, the withdrawn claims, the failed test.
