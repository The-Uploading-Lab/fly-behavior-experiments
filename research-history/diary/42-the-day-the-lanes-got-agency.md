**Question.** What happens when the constraint on a five-lane research
fleet stops being instructions and becomes ownership — each lane told to
disagree in its first line, drop dead routes, and probe blockers twice
before reporting them?

**Method.** Robin issued two directives mid-afternoon: measured structure
enters the model now (per-neuron delays from cable length, graded release
searched, no behaviour targets needing unmodelled mechanisms), and high
agency for every seat. The judge enforced charters, merged only what
replayed bit-identically, and required exact scalar agreement on main
before any selection change.

**Result.** The carried body gained, in order, each verified end to end:
a right-only thorax-coxa phase that fixes fresh-seed turn fragility, a
halved static drift, conductance-based synapses (removing membrane
potentials of -169.8 mV that no chloride reversal allows), and critical
joint damping, which converted a wobble into directed turning. Its first
contact with measured fly quantities passed four bands of five — real
cadence at 6.455 Hz, clean tripod antiphase — and produced one conflict,
duty 0.889 against the fly's 0.50-0.83, kept as measured after the lane
that found it refused to walk the threshold down. The male whole-CNS body
passed every registered gate for one seed and failed its replication at
2 of 4, joining three other one-seed wins that died at their own
preregistered bars: a motor-authority window, a recurrent-gain boost, and
true shunting. The calibration ledger got its second measured row, with
the paper's own control written into it as a warning: the value is
network-state, importing it as intrinsic counts the drive twice.

**Interpretation.** The selected body reads its neurons through a 100 ms
rate integral, so timing mechanisms are structurally inert on it and rate
mechanisms act — measured from both sides in one evening. The duty
conflict lives in the plant's mechanics, not the command waveform, except
through one dial whose secret turned out to be re-timing the phase before
inverse dynamics so the drive matches. Preregistration earned its keep
four times in twelve hours.

**Problems.** The selection moved faster than its own runners twice, and
two gains were measured against stale bases before the exact-agreement
check caught both; the selection now lives as a file runners must build
from. The judge registered one wrong conflict (an electrical synapse
mistaken for a chemical constraint) and withdrew it on a lane's
adversarial pass. Duty is reduced, not closed.

**What changed in the model.** `selection-theta.json`: C-V41 with the
per-class phase, the 2.5 deadband, conductances and critical damping.
