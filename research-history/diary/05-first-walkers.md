The walking battery was born: fixed real-fly bands (step frequency,
swing duration, duty factor, contralateral phase, speed, six joint
ranges, reflex rules), measured on fresh seeds, never widened to make
a conflict disappear. From here on, every fly gets a card and the
cards are comparable forever.

Same night, the invariant-core experiment: keep only connections of
at least 10 synapses — the ones measured as >90% reproducible between
individual flies — and drop the rest. Standing tied (16/30 vs 17/30),
but standing WALKS DOUBLED (median 3.92 vs 1.95 mm, p = 0.0003), 2.4x
faster to simulate. The individual-variability tail wasn't just
noise; it was drag. The core became our standard substrate.

Also certified this day: load prolongs stance (rule R5), our first
behavioural rule matched against fly literature.

**Human:** the standing question — "old truths must be re-measured,
not assumed" — that made the battery re-run forever.
