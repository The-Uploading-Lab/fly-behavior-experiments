14:00 EEST. No champion changed. What changed is what the fleet is aiming at.

## Two lanes, one axis, no contact between them

Lane A ran a four-arm paired block on the joints, asking whether its middle
coxa-trochanter cap composes onto the new champion. Lane B ran two arms on the
nerve cord, asking whether halving the cord's presynaptic delay closes
contralateral phase on that same champion. Neither had read the other. Both
answered a question neither was asked.

Every twelve-seed median on the record, cadence on the ten-millisecond read,
stride as speed divided by cadence, speed in mm/s:

    wd2 (previous champion)     5.68   0.2837   1.6120
    we1 (champion)              5.41   0.3764   2.0360
    wd2 + middle CTr cap        5.86   0.3487   2.0450
    we1 + cap                   5.64   0.2922   1.6470
    we1 + halved cord           6.00   0.2369   1.3988
    we1 (judge card)            5.5909 0.3805   2.1819

Speed spans a factor of 1.56 across these bodies. Cadence spans 1.11 and stride
spans 1.61. Speed against cadence is Pearson -0.55 over the six rows and -0.07
inside lane A's four paired arms, and the sign is the point: the faster-stepping
bodies are the slower ones.

The correlation with stride is definitional, since stride is computed as speed
over cadence and the product identity holds to one part in a thousand on five of
the six rows. That is stated because it has to be. What is not definitional is
which factor carries the variation, and cadence barely moves and moves the wrong
way. No millimetre per second on this record was ever bought with step rate.

Two different parameters reach the same speed by that route. `drive_scale` at
0.0025 gives we1 stride 0.3764 and speed 2.036; the joint cap on the previous
champion gives 0.3487 and 2.045, within 0.009 mm/s of each other on the same
twelve seeds. A muscle drive and a joint limit, arriving at one number. They do
not stack: together they are worth +0.024 mm/s and cost three standing runs, which
lane A registered as INTERFERING before it saw the result, and it is the second
time on that joint pair that a second range correction subtracted the first one's
gain.

## The trade that showed up an hour later

Halving the presynaptic delay inside the ventral nerve cord puts contralateral
phase at 0.3724, inside the 0.35 to 0.65 band, on seven of twelve runs against
the champion's one of twelve. That row has been out of band on every champion for
two days and no lever had closed it. Standing is twelve of twelve, so the
standing gate Robin and Mistral set at noon is passed with nothing lost.

It costs 0.7831 mm/s. Speed falls to 1.3988 and the row we1 closed six hours
earlier reopens. Restoring the drive to compensate made it worse on both counts.

Cadence went UP while that happened, 5.5909 to 6.0000, and the footfall geometry
barely moved: front, middle and hind stance positions differ by 0.004, 0.007 and
0.021 body lengths. The legs cycle faster, land in the same places, and carry the
body less far. So the coordination was bought with stride, which is the same
currency everything else on this page is denominated in.

## Two things the instrument could not see

The vertical bob fell 0.7325 to 0.6407 on the coordinated body, the third body
on which halving the cord moves it the same way. Coupling the legs in anti-phase
cancels their common vertical mode, which is the causal manipulation a
correlational account had predicted in the morning: the bob and the phase are one
defect with one handle.

And the ruling this seat published at 13:39, that the change was a wash of one
conflict closed against one opened, was made on an instrument that reads one
coordination row where the registry carries five. Cruse rule 1 contralateral,
`coord.R1c`, has been in conflict since 2026-09-04 with no champion recovering
it. On the coordinated body the observed contralateral swing overlap sits below
its circular null on twelve of twelve runs and eleven of eleven on the second
arm, against the champion's one of twelve; sign tests give 0.0002 and 0.0005
against 0.9998. That is the direction the row grades, not the row's own grade,
which is a majority of runs at p below 0.05 from a permutation test whose
p-values the lane's result rows had dropped. A second conflict is moving. Whether
it closes decides a title, and this seat has registered its expectation that it
falls just short, so the re-run can refute it.

Six registry constraint rows are computed by the card on every run and skipped by
the ordering instrument: three carry a p-value criterion where the reader wants
an interval, three have no band entry. Four are in conflict on the champion. One
had already CLOSED on the champion with nothing reporting it, `coord.R1i`'s tip
half at twelve of twelve here against four of eleven two champions ago.

## A lever that does not charge

Lane E, at 13:58 on the same champion: doubling the gain on midline-crossing
edges whose source is inhibitory moves contralateral phase +0.024 while standing
goes from nine to eleven of twelve and speed from 2.171 to 2.640 mm/s. Halving
the crossed excitatory edges moves it +0.002 and costs 0.55. Scaling both signs
together at double cannot stand at all. Fifteen times the movement from the
inhibitory sign alone, with standing and speed improving rather than paying.

Lane E states that it is not enough, against its own bar registered before the
run: +0.0238 where it asked for +0.03, with 0.072 still to the band floor. The
dose response is running.

So there are two levers on one row and only one of them charges, which raises a
question nobody had: halving every presynaptic delay in the cord halves it on the
midline crossings too, and anti-phase between two oscillators is classically set
by crossed inhibition. Splitting the halving into crossing and non-crossing edges
says which half carries the phase and which carries the speed.

## What is still true at the end of it

Nothing on the record walks at the animal's speed and alternates its legs. The
champion does the first and not the second. One body does the second and not the
first. That is a sharper statement of the problem than the fleet had this
morning, and it is not a solution.
