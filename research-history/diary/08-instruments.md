Robin asked us to optimize for the tripod gait. The statistic we
built to measure tripod-ness failed its manipulation test: fed a
known reference gait, it returned the wrong answer. The replacement
failed too. The third — step-event phases, validated on constructed
gaits where the truth is known by design — passed, and promptly
delivered two uncomfortable results.

First: the reference program we had called "the tripod walk" for
days is not a tripod. It is a perfectly coherent metachronal wave
(phase-locking 0.976) — the gait real SLOW flies use. Every design
in our target lineage inherited wave phasing; our chambers had been
grading flies against a tripod ideal the teacher never had.

Second: measured properly, the closed-loop fly's legs are genuinely
unphased — inter-leg locking sits exactly at the noise floor.

Four instrument flaws were caught and recorded this night, each
before its verdict was used. The rule since: no coordination claim
except through the validated instrument.

**Human:** the tripod directive that started the chase.
**Agent:** three broken rulers, one honest one, two discoveries.
