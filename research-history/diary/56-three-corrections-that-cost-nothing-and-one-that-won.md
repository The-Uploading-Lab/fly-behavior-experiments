## Why three corrections went to one match

Robin, 2026-09-04: "every neuron should have as realistic a model of it as
possible with zero conflicts with a real fly. We're just trying to copy biology
into our model." Three corrections of that kind sat on the judge's desk by
noon, each a change to a sensory neuron's model, each measured by its lane to
leave the walking count where it was:

- **Lane E, the campaniform channel fed by load.** Since the loop closed on
  2026-08-09 the campaniform sensilla, which report strain, were fed muscle
  activation as a stand-in because the model had no ground. It has had ground
  for weeks. Lane E measured the stand-in at 69% of the channel's drive
  (R366, two seeds, factor 3.2365 with spread 0.036) and the load signal at
  that factor neutral over 24 paired seeds (R367: 9 against 8 qualifying,
  6 wins 5 losses 13 ties).
- **Lane E, modality by exact cell type.** The encoder had read each leg
  sensory row's modality from BANC's `cell_sub_class`. Lane E's R299 audit
  found 71 rows whose exact `cell_type` names a different modality, and R271
  eighteen SNpp39/41 rows in the same class. Lane D ported the seam with five
  tests and asked for it on main; lane E had run every arm on it for a day.
- **Lane A, the front-claw mirror.** BANC types the right front leg's claw
  afferents SNpp50/SNpp51 in the mirror of the left's, the one reversal among
  eleven leg-side comparisons across two animals (Fisher 1.6e-06). Correcting
  the 25 cells moved the front contralateral phase 46 to 63 degrees toward the
  animal's on 36 seeds with the count unchanged (15 against 16).
