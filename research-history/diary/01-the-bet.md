Robin set the frame in one message: a fully closed-loop fly, grounded
in real measurement, judged on behaviour — "behavioural data is king
compared to functional data in the brain. this is a lofi upload not a
full WBE."

We downloaded BANC (188,508 neurons, 13.6 million synapses, one female
fly) and built the scorer: 7 gait metrics validated on ~3,000 real
flies. The rule that the scorer is a frozen instrument — any bad score
is the model's fault, never the metric's — existed from this first day.
Almost nothing else did. There was no battery, no pre-registration, no
parity rule. Those all had to be earned the hard way, and this diary
shows when and how.

**Human:** the entire framing.
**Agent:** the downloads and the scorer.
