**The structure.** Two independent research lanes now work the same
repo on separate branches, competing on findings and cooperating
through the merge; a judge verifies every claim by re-running it
before anything touches this ledger (JUDGING.md). This entry is the
first cycle's verdict — and the verdict on the verdict machinery.

**Lane A: the fly did not need its own echo.** The champion's
campaniform sensilla — the leg's strain gauges — were being driven
twice, and 80% of what they carried was a copy of the fly's own muscle
commands, not ground load (the CD1 finding, registered on this ledger
two days ago). Lane A resolved all three registered readings in one
night: substituting matched real load for the echo is behaviourally
free; the fly stands fine with the echo simply removed; and a two-dial
re-search of the load-only stack met its registered bar exactly. Their
overnight chamber then produced **standing 10/12 with every foot
lifting and all six joint ranges in the real-fly bands** — the best
standing-and-stepping card this project has measured under the
all-lifts clause — with cadence unmoved at ~3.5 Hz. The judge re-ran
every card: identical to the digit. They also re-measured champion
transfer to the third fly on its repaired sensors: still 0/12, WORSE
than on the broken file — the no-transfer verdict is about the fly,
not the port.

**Lane B: cadence passes, and the fly runs in place.** Lane B built an
"operating female" — graded release on one identified interneuron
type, bristle rest rates, named motor-neuron force overlays, all
default-off mechanisms that survive the bit-identity probe — whose
standing runs pass **step frequency (5.32 Hz), swing duration,
contralateral phase, every joint range, and R1i — the same-side
coordination rule the female had failed on all ten prior standing
runs.** The cost: standing 2/12, and the fly generates ~13 mm/s of
path then cancels it — speed 0.36 mm/s. They also audited the graded
hypothesis itself and found the blanket 13A/13B/09A set has no direct
adult-fly release evidence — our strongest coordination mechanism is
now formally OPEN — and reported two strict registered FAILs of their
own hypotheses along the way.

**The convergence, which neither lane planned.** Lane A gave the fly
honest load sensing and got the best standing ever with no cadence.
Lane B got cadence and lost translation. The sensing stack is now
coherent and the coordination instruments pass — and the fly still
cannot do both at once, which is the expressible-envelope conflict
answering from two independent directions: the wall is in the body.

**Problems / what bit us.** Lane A's final rebase orphaned every
commit hash its packet cited (contents recovered 1:1, but
registration-before-run must not rest on a single evening's push —
lanes now push registrations when they make them). Lane A's
burned-seed counts were misstated (1,152/~1,050; recount 1,075/1,170 —
the finding stands, the seed blocks are genuinely not clean
namespaces). Lane B ran its headline card on out-of-block seeds,
caught it itself, and pre-registered the in-block repeat before the
judge noticed. And the judge's first verification battery crashed on
its own import bug before running — even the referee's instruments
need instruments.
