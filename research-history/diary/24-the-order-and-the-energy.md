Since the day the loop closed, one question has outlived every answer
attempted on it: the fly stands, the fly steps, the fly stays. Nineteen
mechanisms died on it. A champion was crowned in the middle of it,
still lost. Tonight it has a mechanism — not a suspect, an account.

To travel, a leg's joints must move in order: hip, then knee, then
ankle, offset by at least ten milliseconds — that number measured by
dissecting the artificial program that does walk, and confirmed on five
controller lineages. The connectome cannot supply it. Not because the
drive is weak: the network's output carries four and a half times the
walking-band power of the working program — the energy argument
inverted overnight into the sharpest premise correction of the
campaign. Because the drive is disordered: signals cross the motor
graph in at most two synaptic hops of 1.8 milliseconds each, and
nothing measured anywhere in the core — no oscillator among 7,300
scorable neurons, no rhythm in 294 motor rows, no periodicity in the
drive tape — stretches that into the tens of milliseconds a gait
requires. The oscillator substrate exists, sixteen thousand neurons of
it, wired and silent. The network sits in the balanced asynchronous
state, and that state — the same one that once saved this model from
seizure — may be what forbids its rhythm. That hypothesis is now
testable, because the one parameter that controls it was unreachable
through an innocent truthiness bug the lane refused to hack around and
the judge fixed instead.

The same day, the model met real flies. Three thousand recorded
windows, the frozen scorer path, no tuning: the model walks inside the
real fly's range on all four gait measures — and misses the real
median on all four. It steps a quarter slow. It is nearly twice as
asymmetric. And where real flies favour the tripod, the model
anti-favours it, beyond its confidence interval, twice. Inside the
room, wrong at the table — which is finally a target with coordinates.

The lane also turned fifteen adversarial agents on itself before the
judge saw the packet, confirmed five of its own defects, withdrew two
published verdicts, and disclosed that its most exciting result — an
artificial half-rhythm blended into the connectome's drive restores
standing, lifting and travel at once — rests on a script that was never
committed and does not rebuild. That result, the one that would close
the story, is therefore not in the ledger tonight. The discipline that
keeps it out is the same discipline that makes everything else in this
entry worth believing.
